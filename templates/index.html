Abstract

This study presents a unified framework for animal emotion detection that integrates robust object detection with cross-species emotion recognition. We propose a multi-stage pipeline that isolates relevant animal regions in real-world images to then apply a deep learning–based emotion recognition module to infer emotional states. We further investigate our model's performance across biologically related species, evaluating whether models trained on select species can generalize to previously unseen animals.
